# Halcyon Ridge Analytics — Employee Onboarding Policy

> DEMONSTRATION DATA. Halcyon Ridge Analytics Pte. Ltd. is a fictional company. This policy was written for the Enterprise Knowledge Assistant demo and does not describe any real organisation.

Policy owner: People & Culture team. Version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains what new employees can expect during their first months at Halcyon Ridge Analytics and what they are expected to complete. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees in Singapore. Contractors and interns follow the separate Contractor Engagement Guide, except where this policy says otherwise.

## 2. Before your first day

Your hiring manager sends a welcome email at least five business days before your start date. The email confirms your start time, your office location and the name of your onboarding buddy.

You must return the signed employment contract, a copy of your identity document and your bank details for payroll through the HR portal before your first day. Payroll cannot be set up without these documents, and salary for the first month may be delayed if they arrive late.

## 3. Your first week

### 3.1 Day one

On day one you will collect your laptop and access badge from the IT service desk on level 4 between 9:00 and 10:00. The People & Culture team runs a 90-minute orientation session at 10:30 that covers company values, benefits and the internal tools you will use.

### 3.2 Onboarding buddy

Every new employee is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team for their first 60 days. The buddy is your first point of contact for informal questions about how things work. Buddies meet new employees at least once a week during the first month.

### 3.3 Mandatory training

New employees must complete the following e-learning modules within their first 10 business days: information security awareness, data protection (PDPA), anti-bribery and the code of conduct. You must acknowledge the code of conduct in the HR portal within 5 business days of your start date.

## 4. Probation

All new employees serve a probation period of three months. Your manager holds a check-in at the end of month one and a formal probation review in the final two weeks of month three. Probation may be extended once, by up to three additional months, if agreed objectives have not been met. During probation, either party may end employment with one week of written notice; after confirmation the notice period is one month.

## 5. Leave

### 5.1 Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 1.5 days per completed month of service, and new employees may take accrued leave during probation with their manager's approval. Up to 5 days of unused annual leave can be carried over into the next year; carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited.

### 5.2 Sick leave

Employees are entitled to 14 days of paid outpatient sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any absence of more than two consecutive days. Notify your manager before 9:30 on each day you are unwell.

### 5.3 Public holidays

Halcyon Ridge observes all Singapore gazetted public holidays. If a public holiday falls on a Saturday, employees receive one day of time off in lieu.

## 6. Equipment and accounts

### 6.1 Laptop

Each employee receives a laptop on day one. You may choose between a 14-inch ThinkPad running Windows 11 and a 13-inch MacBook Air. Laptops are refreshed every three years. Personal software may only be installed from the approved Software Catalogue in the IT portal.

### 6.2 Accounts and multi-factor authentication

Your company account is activated on your first morning. Multi-factor authentication must be set up before you can access email or any internal system. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT service desk as described in the IT Support and Incident Escalation Policy.

## 7. Working arrangements

### 7.1 Hybrid work

Halcyon Ridge uses a hybrid model. Employees work from the office at least two days per week, and Tuesday and Thursday are the team anchor days on which everyone is expected in the office. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 Singapore time.

### 7.2 Working from abroad

Employees may work remotely from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Working from abroad requires written approval from both your manager and the People & Culture team at least 10 business days before travel, so that tax and data protection requirements can be checked.

## 8. Learning and development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of SGD 1,200 for courses, certifications and conferences. Requests above SGD 500 for a single item require manager approval in advance. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

## 9. Expenses

Business expenses must be submitted through the Ledgerly expense portal within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred, with an itemised receipt. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next payroll run or within 10 business days, whichever is sooner. Claims submitted after 30 days require written justification and finance director approval.

## 10. Questions

Questions about this policy can be sent to the People & Culture team through the HR portal. Your onboarding buddy and your manager can also help with day-to-day questions.
