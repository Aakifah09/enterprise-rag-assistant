BEACON INSIGHTS — PRODUCT FAQ FOR SALES AND SUPPORT TEAMS

DEMONSTRATION DATA. Beacon Insights and Halcyon Ridge Analytics are fictional. This FAQ was written for the Enterprise Knowledge Assistant demo; prices, features and terms are invented.

Last reviewed: February 2026. Owner: Product Marketing.


WHAT IS BEACON INSIGHTS?

Beacon Insights is Halcyon Ridge Analytics' cloud analytics platform. It connects to a company's data warehouse, lets business users build dashboards without writing SQL, and sends scheduled reports and anomaly alerts by email or Slack.


PLANS AND PRICING

Q: What plans are available?
A: There are three plans: Starter, Growth and Enterprise.

Q: How much does the Starter plan cost?
A: Starter costs USD 49 per user per month, billed monthly or annually. It supports up to 10 users and includes 50 GB of managed storage.

Q: How much does the Growth plan cost?
A: Growth costs USD 99 per user per month. It includes 250 GB of managed storage, single sign-on (SSO) with SAML 2.0, and role-based access control.

Q: How is the Enterprise plan priced?
A: Enterprise pricing is custom and is quoted by the sales team based on the number of users, storage and support requirements. Enterprise includes a dedicated customer success manager, audit logs and a contractual uptime SLA of 99.95%.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes. New workspaces get a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card is required to start a trial. At the end of the trial the workspace switches to read-only until a plan is purchased.

Q: What is the refund policy?
A: Monthly subscriptions are not refundable. Annual subscriptions can be cancelled within the first 30 days for a prorated refund of the unused months. After 30 days, annual subscriptions are not refundable but remain active until the end of the term.

Q: Do you offer discounts?
A: Registered non-profit organisations and accredited universities receive a 30% discount on Starter and Growth annual plans.


DATA AND INTEGRATIONS

Q: Which data sources can Beacon connect to?
A: Beacon has native connectors for Snowflake, Google BigQuery, Amazon Redshift, PostgreSQL, MySQL and Salesforce. Other sources can be loaded through CSV upload or the REST API.

Q: Where is customer data stored?
A: Customers choose a data residency region when the workspace is created: Singapore, Frankfurt or Northern Virginia. The region cannot be changed after creation without a migration project led by support.

Q: How long is data retained?
A: By default, query results and dashboard snapshots are retained for 13 months. Enterprise customers can configure retention between 30 days and 7 years.

Q: Can data be exported?
A: Yes. Any dashboard dataset can be exported to CSV or Apache Parquet, and all workspace data is available through the REST API.

Q: What are the API rate limits?
A: The REST API allows 600 requests per minute per workspace. Requests above the limit receive HTTP 429 responses and should be retried with exponential backoff.


SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE

Q: Is Beacon certified?
A: Beacon holds a SOC 2 Type II report and ISO/IEC 27001 certification. Customers can request the latest SOC 2 report under NDA through their account manager.

Q: How is data encrypted?
A: Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Enterprise customers can use customer-managed encryption keys.

Q: Does Beacon train AI models on customer data?
A: No. Customer data is never used to train machine-learning models, including the models behind the anomaly-alert feature.


AVAILABILITY AND SUPPORT

Q: What uptime does Beacon commit to?
A: The Growth plan has a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA and the Enterprise plan has a 99.95% monthly uptime SLA. The Starter plan has no contractual SLA. Service credits are issued when the SLA is missed.

Q: What are the support response times?
A: Starter customers receive email support with a first response within 2 business days. Growth customers receive email and chat support with a first response within 8 business hours. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 phone support with a first response within 1 hour for critical issues.

Q: How do customers report an outage?
A: Customers should check the public status page first and then open a ticket marked "Critical" in the support portal. Internally, a customer-reported outage is handled as a P1 incident under the IT Support and Incident Escalation Policy.
